Hello! I am a [postdoctoral research fellow](https://profiles.sydney.edu.au/haejin.lee) in human-centered educational data science in the School of Computer Science at the University of Sydney, working with [Kalina Yacef](https://profiles.sydney.edu.au/kalina.yacef).

I received my Ph.D in Information Sciences from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), where I was advised by 
[Nigel Bosch](https://pnigel.com/) and [Jana Diesner](https://www.gov.sot.tum.de/hcc/home/). 


My research sits at the intersection of human–computer interaction (HCI), eXplainable artificial intelligence (XAI) in education, and the learning sciences. I study how AI can help people think and learn. Specifically, my work focuses on supporting metacognitive engagement by helping people reflect on and regulate their own thinking and learning across various contexts, including STEM education, writing with generative AI, and professional learning. Through my interdisciplinary work, I examine how people interact with and perceive AI-driven learning systems, particularly interactive systems that use XAI to communicate the reasoning behind their learning recommendations.  

I approach this work through three complementary research goals: 
           
1. Modeling self-regulated learning in computer-based learning environments using learning analytics
              
2. Designing and developing explainable AI systems to support self-regulated learning

3. Assessing the fairness of these AI systems, particularly for underserved learners (e.g., individuals diagnosed with ADHD). 

Before beginning my PhD, I earned my B.S. in Mathematics and Statistics at UIUC. Outside of research, I love walking my dog and exploring new places when I travel.
